My Project

Quantum sequential decision-making

Project overview

The project involves theoretical and algorithmic analysis for quantum sequential decision-making problems. It has two main objectives:

  1. Find lower and upper bounds on the complexity of approximately optimal quantum decision-making algorithms.
  2. Propose quantum algorithms with optimal complexity.

Through 16.S893, this portfolio will document how I use LLMs to support the research and how I evaluate their contributions.
